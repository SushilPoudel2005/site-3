welcome!

Soil Stewards is the University of Idaho's student-run certified organic farm — three acres on Farm Road where students learn sustainable agriculture by doing the work themselves.

Started in 2003, the club is focused on local, organic and sustainable farming for students and the surrounding community. Members grow starts for the Farmer's Market and produce sold through CSA shares and on-farm sales — gaining hands-on experience in farming, project development, and organization along the way.

Participation is flexible, because the farm's needs are multifaceted. Everyone is welcome; everyone has something they can bring to the table.
