about us

Local, organic, and student-run since 2003 — a club focused on sustainable farming for students and the surrounding community of Moscow, Idaho.

Our story Timeline What's next Our director
How it started

A class project that took root

In 2003, an environmental philosophy class at the University of Idaho was assigned to research sustainability among agriculture across campus. They found a lot of capacity, but little being done in terms of growing food.

At the same time, CALS soil scientist Jodi Johnson-Maynard had a group of students who wanted a club or project to participate in. Together, these two groups created an organic student farm — and the student participants became known as the Soil Stewards.

Our timeline

Two decades of growing

  1. 2003

    The farm is born

    Started on about three acres of transitional organic land within the larger University of Idaho Parker Farm. Early on, the Soil Stewards ran a Community Supported Agriculture program, sold at an on-campus farm stand, and sold produce to the university's food service provider.

  2. 2015

    A new home on Farm Road

    The farm moved to a three-acre parcel on Farm Road near the U of I Sheep Center — closer to campus, so we could engage more with the student body.

  3. 2020

    Certified organic

    Still a mixed vegetable farm, the operation earned its organic certification.

  4. Today

    21+ years and counting

    Members grow starts for the Farmer's Market and produce sold through CSA shares and on-farm sales — gaining experience in farming, project development, and organization along the way. We're here to teach and to learn.

Looking ahead

What's growing next

Hoop house

Finishing construction on a hoop house to extend our growing season — potentially enabling year-round operation at the farm.

Weather station

Adding a weather station on-site to build hands-on data and skill-building opportunities for students.

Water monitoring

Expanding water monitoring through agricultural technology, integrated directly into our farm operations.
