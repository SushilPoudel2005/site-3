Get Dirty work sessions

Thursdays, 4:00–6:00 PM at the Soil Stewards Student Farm.

Drop-in work sessions open to anyone — students, faculty, and community members alike. No experience needed, no commitment required. Come learn to plant, weed, harvest, and everything in between.

Work-for-share program

Put in five hours a week at the farm and receive a weekly share of produce in return. Open to students from every college and major, and to community members too.

The club is the main driver of day-to-day farm work — from planting starts in the greenhouse to harvesting through spring, summer, and fall.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any farming experience?

Not at all. Get Dirty sessions are made for learning — we'll show you what to do.

Who can volunteer?

Anyone! Students from every college and major, U of I faculty and staff, and community members are all welcome.

Do I have to commit to every week?

No commitment is required for Get Dirty sessions — come when you can. If you'd like something regular, the work-for-share program asks for five hours a week in exchange for a weekly share of produce.

What should I wear or bring?

Clothes and closed-toe shoes you don't mind getting dirty, sun protection, and a water bottle.

Where is the farm?

425 Farm Rd, Moscow, ID 83843 — near the U of I Sheep Center. See the map on our contact page.
